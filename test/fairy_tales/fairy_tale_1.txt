Once there was a little cloud named Puff who looked at the other clouds. They rained water to help flowers grow, but Puff wanted to rain gold coins to help the poor. He tried very hard, but only gray water came out. He felt sad and cried. A little girl saw him and said, "Your water makes the flowers grow, and those flowers can be sold for gold." Puff realized his water was magic too. He rained gently, and a garden of golden flowers bloomed for everyone.
