A little fox met a wise old owl one night. The fox asked, "How do I become brave?" The owl hooted and pointed to a dark cave. "Go inside and bring back a feather." The fox was scared but went in. He found a feather on the ground and ran out. The owl smiled and said, "You were brave enough to go in, even though you were scared." The fox learned that bravery is acting even when you are afraid.
